Our story

A tender beginning, a growing purpose.

Tender Hearts Initiative is a safe space where vulnerable people can be nurtured spiritually, grow through vulnerability, find healing, and become stronger, self-loving people.

How it began

Compassion at the heart of it.

Established in June 2023, the initiative was born out of compassion and care for vulnerable and helpless people, including those who feel hopeless.

Its early work began with orphanage outreaches and online platform activities where people encouraged one another and prayed together. The former name was Daughters of Zion; today, Tender Hearts Initiative carries forward its purpose of giving life, hope, encouragement, and support.

It is intended to be a safe space for spiritual growth, vulnerability, healing, mentorship, and personal development.

Our mission

“To nurture tender hearts by putting spiritual growth first, while allowing vulnerability and allowing growth through mentorship on sexual purity, self-love, personal development and healing.”

Our vision

“A world where children, teenagers, girls, women and communities who are by nature tender-hearted and vulnerable, are nurtured spiritually and allowed to grow into strong, pure and self-loving people who impact their world.”
