A shared mission

Nurturing tender hearts, together.

Tender Hearts Initiative exists to nurture vulnerable people through spiritual growth, vulnerability, mentorship, healing, and support.

Across communities

Care that reaches further.

The initiative works with children, teenagers, girls, women, widows, and communities across Ekiti State, Ogun State, Lagos State, and beyond.
