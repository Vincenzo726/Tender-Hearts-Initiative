Programs & outreach

Care, expressed in community.

Through outreach and personal growth, Tender Hearts Initiative creates space for encouragement, spiritual growth, healing, and support.

Outreach

Showing up where people are.

School and orphanage activities, alongside foundation initiatives, are central ways we put compassion into action.

01 · School Outreach

School outreach offers an opportunity to connect with children and teenagers through encouragement and care. It is part of the initiative's broader work nurturing spiritual growth, personal development, and self-love.

Support the work

02 · Orphanage Outreach

Orphanage outreach was part of the initiative's beginnings and remains a key activity. It reflects the foundation's commitment to offering care, hope, encouragement, and support to vulnerable people.

Visit the gallery

03 · Foundation Activities

Foundation activities create space for practical care, encouragement, and support among the people and communities served by Tender Hearts Initiative. These efforts continue to grow with sensitivity and intentional care.
